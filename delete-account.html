Request account deletion

Cab Book is provided by Buraq Technologies. You can request deletion here even if you no longer have the app installed.

What will be deleted

After we verify that the request is from the account owner, we will delete the Cab Book sign-in account and its saved profile, rates, and shifts. This removes your shift history and calculated earnings. Deletion cannot be undone. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete this online data.

Send your request

Use the email address associated with your Cab Book account. Include that account email in your message so we can identify the correct records. We may reply to verify ownership before deleting anything.

Open email to request deletion

Your email app will open a draft. Tap Send there to submit the request. If no email app opens, write to namr.dev08@gmail.com with the subject “Cab Book account deletion request”. You do not need to reinstall Cab Book.

Questions

For other privacy questions, contact namr.dev08@gmail.com. Read the Cab Book Privacy Policy for details about data use and retention.
