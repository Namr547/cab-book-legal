Cab Book Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

Cab Book helps taxi drivers record shifts and calculate earnings. This Privacy Policy explains the information Cab Book handles and how to ask for its deletion. Cab Book is provided by Buraq Technologies.

Information you provide

When you create or use an account, we handle your email address, display name, and any profile photo or phone number supplied by your sign-in method. We store your account ID and time zone with your profile. You enter your rates and shift information, including dates, income amounts, kilometres, trip counts, tolls, and any saved note. Cab Book stores the calculated earnings and rate snapshot with each shift.

How we use it

We use account information to sign you in and show your saved records. We use rates and shift information to calculate daily clean money, show your shift history, and prepare dashboard totals and averages. A support email you send us is used to answer your request.

Services involved

Google Firebase Authentication handles email/password and Google sign-in. Firebase Firestore stores account profiles, rates, and shifts. Firebase Analytics is included in the app and may collect app usage and device information. Google Fonts may download and cache font files on your device. These Google services handle information under their own privacy terms.

Storage on your device

Cab Book caches some rates on your device so they can be shown when the network is unavailable. Firebase may also keep local copies of recently used records for offline access. Removing the app or clearing its data removes these local copies from that device, but does not by itself delete your Firebase account or saved online records.

Public policy pages

Our public privacy and account-deletion pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log a visitor's IP address for site security under its own privacy terms.

Sharing and advertising

Your records are processed by the services above to run Cab Book. The current app does not show ads, sell your shift records, or send them to a taxi company or Careem. If these practices change, we will update this policy and the app disclosures before that release.

Security

Cab Book uses Firebase Authentication for sign-in. Firebase services transfer data over encrypted connections and store the account records used by the app. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account, rates, and shifts while your account is active. In Profile, choose Request account deletion to email us. You can also request deletion from this website without installing the app. We verify that the request is from the account owner before deleting the Firebase account and its associated profile, rates, and shifts. Deletion cannot be undone. Service providers may retain operational records under their own policies. Uninstalling Cab Book alone does not delete online account data.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email namr.dev08@gmail.com. We may update this policy if Cab Book changes. The updated policy will show a new effective date.
